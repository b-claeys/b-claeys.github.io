I designed and built an ESP32-controlled environmental chamber to reproduce target temperature, humidity, airflow, and surface-wetness conditions from live weather-station data or manually defined setpoints. The system was developed with a farmer to reduce repeated field visits during weather-sensitive crop-disease testing by reproducing outdoor conditions in a remotely monitored chamber.

Field-Matching Test

Dashboard and chamber response during a field-matching test, showing target conditions, measured conditions, and actuator response.

Project Overview

Sensing + Targets Temperature, relative humidity, and soil moisture measured inside the chamber and compared against live Ecowitt weather-station data or manually defined target values.
Actuation Peltier cooling, resistive heating, blower and circulation fans, a humidifier, and water-delivery pump used to regulate temperature, humidity, airflow, and surface wetness.
Mechanical Design Clear dual-wall enclosure with an insulating air gap, silicone and weather-strip sealing, and a magnetic door closure. A plenum mixes conditioned air before chamber entry, with vent placement directing flow across the upper test volume and away from the soil.
Electrical + Software An ESP32 switches separate 12 V actuator branches through MOSFETs and shared power distribution. The control logic prevents simultaneous heating and cooling, applies timed humidifier and pump pulses with lockout periods, rejects invalid sensor readings, updates the dashboard, and logs test data.

CAD Design

Vents + Airflow Path

Vent and plenum geometry used to route conditioned air from the thermal module into the chamber and distribute it across the upper test volume.

Full Chamber

Dual-wall enclosure with silicone and weather-strip sealing, magnetic door closure, clear sample access, and space for the thermal, humidification, water-delivery, and sensing hardware.

Testing + Validation

System tests measured how chamber temperature, humidity, and surface wetness changed in response to each actuator. I used these runs to tune activation timing and pulse duration, add heating and cooling mutual exclusion, and set humidifier and pump lockout periods so the chamber approached target conditions without repeated overshoot. The control sequence also includes a startup period before field matching begins, allowing the sensors and chamber conditions to stabilize before tracking.

System Response

Field-matching run: mean absolute error of 0.28°C for temperature and 1.94 percentage points for relative humidity over the 77.2-minute settled window. Pump testing recorded the corresponding surface-wetness response after water delivery.
